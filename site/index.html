The look of Nautilus.
The power of its extensions.

Odysseus is a fast file manager for GNOME. It looks and behaves like GNOME Files, and comes with twenty extensions built in: dual panel, Miller columns, an archive browser, content search & replace, PDF tools and more. Every one of them can be switched off in a click.

Views Ctrl+1Ctrl+2Ctrl+3

List, grid or columns. Whatever fits the folder.

Compact Nautilus-style list rows, a thumbnail grid for your photos, and Finder-style Miller columns to dive deep into a tree without losing your way.

✓Optional list columns — size, type, date, permissions, owner, and media duration.
✓Thumbnails shared with Nautilus, through the standard freedesktop cache.
✓Preview panel F4 for images, videos, PDF pages and text, with detailed info.
✓Folder colours and emblems, compatible with Folder Color.
Dual Panel F3

Two folders side by side. Half the clicks.

Move files between two places without juggling windows. Each pane keeps its own view, history and selection, and the operations bar is always one click away.

✓Copy or move to the other pane with F6 / Shift+F6, or drag and drop.
✓Switch panes with Ctrl+Tab, keep tabs, history and bookmarks.
✓A progress window for long copies, with undo for every operation.
✓A pane follows its folder when you rename or move it elsewhere.
Search & Replace F8

Find and replace across files. With a real preview.

Search inside file contents with ripgrep (or grep), then preview every change line by line before a single byte is written.

✓Colored diff preview — old text struck through in red, the new one in green.
✓Per-line checkboxes — apply only what you actually want.
✓Automatic .bak backup, never written over an older one.
✓Plain text or regex, case, whole word, hidden files, extensions filter.
Archives F7

Open an archive like a folder.

Browse more than fifty formats without extracting first, drag files out, add or rename entries. The engine comes from Pressoir and is powered by 7-Zip.

✓ZIP, 7z, RAR, TAR.*, ISO and many more, multi-volume (.001, .002…) included.
✓Smart extraction — one item lands next to the archive, several get their own folder.
✓Compress to 7z, ZIP, tar.zst… with AES-256 encryption, volumes and levels.
✓Trapped archives defused — nothing is ever written outside the destination.
PDF tools

Compress, merge, watermark, rearrange.

The four PDF extensions, rewritten and improved. Every tool shows what it will do before it does it, and never writes over an existing file.

✓Compress with Ghostscript: page-by-page progress, before / after sizes.
✓Merge in any order, with page ranges per file (1-3,7,10-).
✓Watermark with text or image, live preview, optional flattening.
✓Pages — extract, delete, rotate or split, with thumbnails.
Your theme

Looks like your desktop, not like an app.

Odysseus has no colours of its own: it follows your GTK4 theme, light or dark, with your accent colour — Adwaita or a theme installed in ~/.config/gtk-4.0. Dialogs are real windows, so transparency and borders apply too.

✓Light and dark, switched live with the system.
✓Made on Zorin OS, tested on X11, GNOME 46, GTK 4.14.
✓Fully translated in English, French and German.

Preferences → Features

Everything built in. Nothing forced on you.

Each extension is a switch. Turn off what you don't use and it disappears from menus, toolbars and shortcuts — instantly, no restart.

✓No Python extension to install, nothing to restart, nothing to break on updates.
✓Missing tool? The feature tells you which package to install.
✓Duplicates merged — one archive tool, one progress window, one manager.
This PC · Your desktop

Feels at home. Even if home was Windows.

An optional This PC page gathers your folders and every drive, with a usage bar and the free space of each. And Odysseus plugs into GNOME the way Nautilus does.

✓“Show in folder” from Firefox or Thunderbird opens Odysseus — even when it is closed.
✓Send to your phone with GSConnect, or by email with your files attached.
✓Run scripts in a terminal that stays open, straight from the context menu.
✓Zoom with Ctrl+wheel, a status bar that fades under the pointer — the little Nautilus touches.

Names first

The folder is read in the background; names appear at once, sizes and dates follow.

Virtual lists

Only the rows on screen exist. A 100,000-file folder costs what a screenful costs.

What you see, first

Thumbnails and folder sizes are computed for what you are looking at before anything else.

Your files come first

Tested to never lose a byte.

Every destructive operation is tested on throwaway trees, next to a “precious” folder that must never change — including real FAT USB keys and booby-trapped archives.

🔁

Replace is atomic

The new copy is written under a temporary name, synced to disk, then swapped in. If anything fails, the old file is still there.

🚚

Moves only remove what was copied

Skipped, failed or cancelled items stay at the source, between disks too.

💾

USB keys understood

FAT, exFAT, NTFS: forbidden characters cleaned, case clashes renamed, files over 4 GB refused before writing.

🛡️

Nothing is overwritten silently

Archives, PDF outputs, backups: an existing file is never replaced without asking — you get “name (2)”.

🧨

Trapped archives defused

../ paths, absolute paths, links pointing outside: nothing escapes the destination folder.

↩️

Undo that doesn't bite

Undo and redo never overwrite a file created in the meantime.
